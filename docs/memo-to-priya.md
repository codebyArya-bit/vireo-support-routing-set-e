# Priya Raman — decide on routing before headcount

**To:** Priya Raman, Head of Customer Experience, Vireo Audio  
**From:** Aryabrat Mishra  
**Subject:** Four-week routing pilot before committing the next two hires  
**Data:** January 2025–June 2026; latest complete quarter April–June 2026

Please hold the two-hire allocation until we test a routing fix and review capacity. Billing's intake queue overstates the work that remains with Billing. Of 2,425 tickets initially assigned there, 696 were ultimately recorded against Logistics agents. Across the period, Logistics has 2,574 recorded-agent tickets versus Billing's 1,798. Chat Frontline is larger than both. Volume alone cannot decide where hires belong.

Logistics also shows a warning signal: median elapsed resolution is 26.03 hours versus Billing's 1.22 hours, and surveyed satisfaction averages 2.89/5 versus 3.41/5. Survey responses cover only part of the queue. These figures include customer and courier waiting; they do not prove an agent shortage. Tier 2 warranty work must be assessed separately.

**Proposed goal:** halve eligible Billing-to-Logistics detours from **3.26% to 1.63% of all tickets**. In April–June, 77 of 2,364 tickets met a conservative definition: a delivery opening message, initial Billing, completed status, recorded Logistics resolver and at least one known hand-off. I individually checked those 77 texts with AI assistance; no clear non-delivery case emerged. This is not independent human validation.

At one avoided hand-off for half those cases, the policy's Rs 305 transfer cost gives **Rs 11,743 gross savings per quarter at observed volume**. At the brief's 650 tickets/week, the same mix gives **Rs 41,973 gross per quarter**. The export has less volume than that scenario. If all uncertain messages require an extra minute of review, illustrative review effort reduces the latter figure to about **Rs 33,184**, before implementation and machine costs. Prevention and review time are assumptions, not realised savings. The Rs 9 lakh/year hiring expense is not counted as saved.

The tool supplies the requested monthly category/team charts, with separate initial-routing and recorded-agent views. It keeps uncertain cases visible. On a frozen 100-ticket assistant-labelled check, opening-only classification accepted 61 cases, with three wrong and 39 requiring review. With closing notes, it accepted 85, with one wrong and 15 requiring review; that view is retrospective. Order/address changes are a known failure. Vireo staff should adjudicate labels before operational use.

Run a supervised four-week pilot: separate “payment taken, no order created” from “order exists, parcel not delivered”; review 30 routed cases weekly; measure actual avoided hand-offs, first-response breaches and Logistics waiting. Route ambiguous cases through human triage. Finance should confirm realised savings. Then review productive hours, shift coverage and courier causes to decide whether Logistics needs capacity. The tool does not automatically award hires.
