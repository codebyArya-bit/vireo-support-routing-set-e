# Vireo Audio Support Tickets - Set E

Vireo operates a 44-agent support desk across Bengaluru and Indore over chat, email, callbacks and social. The client requests automated ticket categories and monthly category/team charts, proposing two hires for the busiest team.

Inputs comprise 18 months of tickets (Jan 2025 to Jun 2026), assignment rosters, order/customer/product references, policy costs and an email thread. Required outputs are a runnable AI-assisted tool, a numeric business goal and money estimate, validation evidence, a one-page business memo, a screen recording of at most three minutes, and completed submission answers. The brief sets a five-hour effort cap within a 48-hour window.

This tool retains the charts and recommends a supervised routing pilot before hiring decisions. Synthetic examples and aggregate results are public; original customer records are excluded. See README.md for commands and docs/validation.md for limitations.
